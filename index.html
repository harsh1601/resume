Leadership

What I Lead

Technology Strategy & Architecture

I set the architectural direction for national-scale platforms, make build-versus-buy decisions, and guide deployment across data centres and cloud environments. I plan for peak demand, resilience and long-term modernisation.

Organisations & Delivery

I lead delivery across internal engineering teams, vendors and integration partners. I set priorities, coordinate releases across organisations, and mentor engineering leaders.

Ecosystems & Interoperability

I lead the architecture and operation of the GST API ecosystem. It connects government agencies, financial institutions, payment networks and GST Suvidha Providers, enabling them to build services that use GST system capabilities.

Business Process Engineering

I work with tax administration leaders to translate GST rules into system requirements. Together, we define the steps taxpayers and officers follow and the checks the GST System performs.

Selected Work

Programmes I’ve Shaped and Deliver

GSTN · 2016 to present

GST System: India's Indirect Tax Platform

Architecture lead for the world's largest open-source-stack indirect tax platform, including the unified back office used by tax officers across states, refund processing and the post go-live service levels for the managed service provider.

Outcome: concurrency grown from 60,000 to 3 lakh+, with 29 states onboarded without fault and 3 more migrated within 21 days.
GSTN · Ecosystem

e-Invoicing and GST API Ecosystem

Architected India’s B2B GST e-invoicing system and built the wider GST API ecosystem, integrating invoice registration with tax returns and e-way bills and connecting government, financial institutions and industry to GST services.

Outcome: 1.2 crore e-invoices reported daily on average across six authorised registration portals
Tata Consultancy Services

Passport Seva

Led solution architecture and engineering at TCS for Passport Seva, an early flagship programme under India’s National e-Governance Plan. The platform connected passport offices, state police and India Post across the application, verification and delivery process.

Outcome: The nationwide rollout brought passport services closer to citizens through 77 Passport Seva Kendras across 63 cities by June 2012
Aricent · Telecom O/BSS

Integrated BSS and Analytics Programme

Led solution delivery for a cross-shore programme, moved execution to Scrum-based continuous delivery, and productised the solution for new geographies.

Outcome: $6.5M+ ACV, customer satisfaction from 3/5 to 5/5 in 18 months, and $1.3M of new business.
