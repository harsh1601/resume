# LinkedIn Publishing Workflow

For each website article, prepare an abridged LinkedIn version and link readers to the full article.

## Recommended LinkedIn Structure

1. Hook: one short opening line that frames the problem or insight.
2. Context: 2-3 sentences adapted from the article introduction.
3. Key points: 3-5 concise bullets or short paragraphs.
4. Takeaway: one practical closing thought.
5. Link: `Read the full article: https://agrharsh.com/blog/articles/<article-slug>.html`

## Suggested Tracking Link

Use a LinkedIn-specific URL when you want analytics attribution:

`https://agrharsh.com/blog/articles/<article-slug>.html?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=blog`

## Publishing Control

Posting to LinkedIn should be manual or done only after explicit approval, because it publishes externally under the LinkedIn profile.
